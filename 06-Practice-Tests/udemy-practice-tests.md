## Get More Value From Every Test

1. Record your score, completion time, and uncertain answers after each attempt.
2. Map every missed question to the relevant CCA-F domain.
3. Review the official documentation before attempting the next test.
4. Retake only after you can explain the underlying architectural trade-off.
5. Keep several tests unseen for realistic, timed practice near exam day.
